JevFeed

A feed for one person. The seeds are your own browser history; the candidates are links you have not read yet, taken from the body of those pages. Jev reads every candidate at once and puts the best ten in your feed.

No buttons. How fast you scroll, how long you linger, what you open and how long you read it: the last 150 of those go, unchanged, into the next batch. Reach the bottom and ten more arrive. Your history is read locally and never uploaded.
